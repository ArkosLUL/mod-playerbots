# Twin Val'kyr

Raid-wide facts and the code layout: [README.md](README.md).

Shared health pool. Essence-colour assignment. Match the Vortex's colour before its 8 s cast ends;
spreading does nothing. Heroic Touch lands on a player holding the opposite essence, never on either
twin's current victim, and is cleared by switching to the Touch's colour. Collect orbs of your own
colour.
