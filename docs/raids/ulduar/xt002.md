# XT-002

Ulduar is 10/25-man only, so **"hard mode" here is the Heartbreak split, not a difficulty flag**.
Killing the Heart in its 30 s exposed window (63849) sets XT to full health, grants **Heartbreak for
good** and stops rescheduling the phase check: no Heart phase follows. Heartbreak reliably shows hard
mode is live, but nothing shows it *before* the kill, so the config declares intent: on, bots burn the
Heart to zero in the first window; off, they stop at `ULDUAR_XT002_HEART_SAFE_HP_PCT` (15%) so the
fight stays normal.

**Bloodlust goes on the Heart in hard mode.** `XT002BurstWindowMultiplier` releases every burst, lust
included, on the exposed Heart: without lust the 25-man raid killed it in its first window in only 2
of 12 test pulls. Heartbreak then runs under Exhaustion (user call, 2026-11-21). Below
`ULDUAR_XT002_FINAL_PUSH_HP_PCT` (25%) with no Heartbreak both verdicts release: the Heart outlived
all three windows.

**A pile summons only while XT stands outside its gate** (`npc_xt_toy_pile::SpellHit`): 90 yd
(`SummonDistance`) plus both combat reaches (XT's is 15, core `2026_11_06_01.sql`), in 3D. In traces,
piles 98-105 yd from XT never summoned and 108 yd or further did. Each Heart window's energy orbs hit
a random pile, which summons a Boombot, 5-7 Scrapbots and, 30% of the time, a Pummeller, at most every
12 s per pile. The four piles (core `2026_07_04_03.sql`) sit at NE (898, 67), SE (898, -89), SW
(793, -95) and NW (793, 65). With XT on the tank spot the east piles are 73-84 yd off and the west
ones 117-126, so **every add comes from the west**, 54-92 over two windows; near (843, -4) every pile
is inside the gate and none summons. Tank positioning ignores the gate on purpose: add handling works
wherever adds spawn. **No adds come after Heartbreak**: piles summon only when hit by the Heart's
energy orb, and `RescheduleEvents` omits `EVENT_PHASE_CHECK` once `_hardMode`.

**The west piles stand inside the Scrapyard trash** (Parts Recovery Technician 34267, XR-949
Salvagebot 34269, XD-175 Compactobot 34271, XB-488 Disposalbot 34273, eight per side). A Pummeller
spawning within 10 yd (centre to centre) of one calls assistance on engaging, and 2 s later the whole
pack engages its target. Ranged never target the trash and melee only briefly, so it kills freely: 7
of 12 25-man hard mode pulls with the trash alive, all wipes, 98 deaths to trash. The strategy
assumes the trash is cleared, as it is on the walk to XT.

**A kill is not a hard mode kill.** At 25-man the Heart has 9.36M against the raid's ~300k/s, so the
first window leaves it at 4-10% (trash cleared, 3 of 3 pulls), and XT dies to the damage it passes on
within three windows: a normal kill with hard mode configured. Call a kill hard mode by Heartbreak,
never the config.

Boombots explode for 15-18k on reaching XT **or at 50% health**, so melee never touch them. Only
melee dodge them, by `ULDUAR_XT002_BOOMBOT_AVOID_RADIUS` (12 yd, Boom reaching about 10); ranged stand
outside the blast already and target one only from beyond that radius. Scrapbots walk to XT and heal
him, so they die en route.

**An add is engaged only once it leaves its pile** (`IsXT002AddEngageable`): piles sit 78-125 yd from
XT and an add that never paths away stays there, so adds past `ULDUAR_XT002_ADD_LEASH_RADIUS` (60) of
XT are ignored. None needs fetching: Scrapbots and Boombots walk to XT, Pummellers chase whoever they
aggro. Life Sparks are exempt, spawning on the carrier. Each bot also takes only adds within
`_RANGED_ENGAGE_RANGE` (35, enough to reach the Searing Light spot from a slot) or
`_MELEE_ENGAGE_RANGE` (15) of itself; tanks skip that, since the tank XT isn't on fetches the
Pummeller.

**Void Zone and Life Spark are gated on the Heartbreak aura, not the config.** Both spawn from an
`AfterEffectRemove` handler checking `xt002->HasAura(aurEff->GetAmount())`, the Heartbreak aura
(`spell_xt002_gravity_bomb_aura`, `spell_xt002_searing_light_spawn_life_spark` in `boss_xt002.cpp`).
So anything reacting to either keys off `IsXT002HeartbreakActive`, which reads that aura;
`IsXT002HardModeActive` is the *config* flag, right only where the code states intent before the
Heart dies, such as the 15% floor.

**`aurEff->GetAmount()` is the difficulty-correct id**, so read Heartbreak through
`GetXT002HeartbreakSpellId`, never a constant. Hardcoded to the 10-man 65737 it reads false for a
whole 25-man pull, silently taking the parking lot, both Void Zone filters and the burst window with
it while puddles and sparks spawn normally; the trace tell is burst-window vetoes firing long after
the Heart died.

All three difficulty-mapped ids (Searing Light 63018 / 65121, Gravity Bomb 63024 / 64234, Heartbreak
65737 / 64193, 10 / 25-man) go through `sSpellMgr->GetSpellIdForDifficulty`, keyed off the **map**,
never the bot's `GetRaidDifficulty()`
([difficulty ids](../../engine/pitfalls.md#detection-that-never-fires)): that read had all three
helpers answer 10-man in a 25-man pull, so no carrier left the raid, and Consumption did 923k against
46k for a healthy pull with twice the bombs.

**Searing Light and Gravity Bomb each repeat on a 16 s (25-man) / 20 s (10-man) timer**, longer than
the debuff lasts, so there is never more than one carrier of either type at a time.

XT spawns at `(886.28, -12.05, 409.6)` facing −x (orientation 3.13), the room's only DB-spawned
creature; every add is script-summoned, so the world DB can't check room geometry. Verify a
destination with navprobe, never `IsWithinLOS` (below).

`NPC_XT002` (33293), `NPC_XT_TOY_PILE` (33337), `NPC_XS013_SCRAPBOT` (33343) and
`NPC_HEART_OF_DECONSTRUCTOR` (33329) come from core `ulduar.h` via `UldScripts.h` — **do not
redeclare them.**

Use the `IsBurstCooldownAction` registry rather than hand-rolling a `dynamic_cast` list the way
BT and SWP do.

**The 15% Heart floor has two halves**: the priority action stops new picks, and
`XT002TargetGuardMultiplier` blocks hits on the Heart, and only those (a wider block leaves a bot no
heals and no way to retarget), from bots already on it. The floor holds whenever an exposed Heart is
up; the multiplier's stand-downs wait for XT's combat, since encounter nodes don't run before the
pull. It and `XT002BurstWindowMultiplier` veto every action family, items included: the burst list
holds trinkets and potions, and the floor stops anything that would land a hit.

## Targets

`xt002 set dps priority` owns every role's target while XT is up.

- **Non-tank order**: hard mode puts the Heart first, since the mode is decided in its 30 s window;
  then Life Spark (Static Charged stays on the raid until it dies), Scrapbot, Boombot (ranged only),
  Pummeller, the Heart in normal mode, XT. The Heart is skipped while a Life Spark lives.
- **A tank gets XT only**, plus the Pummeller for the tank XT isn't on and, in hard mode, the Heart
  (XT is submerged the whole window). Normal mode keeps tanks off the Heart: a stray tank hit is
  exactly what flips the raid. Generic smart tank targeting ranks every add the tank has no aggro on
  above XT, so a tank left to it walks into the add pile and XT follows.
- The current target holds unless something strictly more urgent is up, so Scrapbot churn can't
  reset swing and cast timers. Within a type the add nearest the bot wins, with a 10 yd switch margin,
  since adds come from both flanks. No `dps target` fallback: it has no leash and hands back the add
  at a toy pile; an empty list means XT is submerged, and the anchor takes over.
- **The trigger is combat-gated**: the action calls `Attack()` directly, so without the gate the first
  bot to see XT pulls him. Whoever pulls flips it for everyone, so the raid engages together.
- XT and the Heart are read off `"nearest npcs"`: both go unselectable for part of the fight, which
  `AttackersValue::IsPossibleTarget` rejects, and the Heart never attacks, so no threat list holds it.
  Each `GetXT002` call rescans (a grid visit plus a LOS ray per npc), so XT is looked up once per
  evaluation and passed down.
- The generic pickers stand down; `attack rti target` stays live so a player's mark still wins.
- The Pummeller taunt fires only inside taunt range, not the leash: at `ACTION_RAID + 4`, firing at an
  add out of reach would burn the tick while a nearer one goes untaunted. Taunt sits above targeting so
  add control runs through the Heart window; positioning is lowest, since an add wave's priority action
  starves it and killing a Scrapbot beats standing on a spot.
- **Threat redirects follow the tank roles** (`xt002 redirect threat`): to the Pummeller tank while a
  Pummeller is inside `ULDUAR_XT002_ADD_LEASH_RADIUS` (one still at its pile is nobody's), else to the
  holder. A rogue's Tricks moves everything for 6 s, so needs no dump shot; a hunter spends
  Misdirection's three shots on XT with Steady Shot, never on the rotation's pick. Off while XT is
  submerged: he is `REACT_PASSIVE` and on no threat list, so a redirect burns its charges on nothing.

## Anchors, and why nothing pre-positions

The tank spot and the ranged anchor `(866.0, -12.5, 409.8)` stay; the pre-pull walk to them does not.
The ranged anchor is pulled in from the first-measured spot, which left 30 yd casters out of range.
`XT002RaidPositionTrigger` requires `xt002->IsInCombat()`, and the generic-mover stand-down carries the
same gate, so a ranged bot near XT before the pull can still follow its master.

- **Tank duties follow whoever XT is on, never the main tank flag** (`GetXT002TankRoles`, user call
  2026-11-22): that tank holds the tank spot, the other takes the Pummellers, through every steal and
  tank death. XT is a vehicle, taunt-immune through `Vehicle::ApplyAllImmunities`, so only damage
  moves him: with the spot tied to the flag, 4 of 11 25-man test pulls had the off-tank's opener land
  while the main tank walked to the spot, and XT stayed 45-55 yd west, by the formation and the Searing
  Light spot, until the kill or the off-tank's death.
  In the Heart window XT has no victim, so his threat list's pick (`GetLastVictim`) names the holder;
  with XT on a non-tank, the tank with most threat on him; before the pull, the flagged one. Fallbacks
  take the first living tank ranked like `GetGroupAssistTank` (flagged main tank, raid assistants, the
  rest); the Pummellers go to the first that isn't the holder. The anchor yields while XT's victim is
  anyone else, matching Ignis, so that tank can damage him back.
  `xt002 taunt guard` blocks the class "lose aggro" taunts at XT: a paladin cast Righteous Defense into
  him every 8 s, a DK Death Grip.
- **DPS hold their damage near the holder's threat** (`IsXT002ThreatBraked`, `xt002 threat brake`). XT
  stays on whoever pulls him until a tank's threat passes theirs: in 9 of 12 test pulls he went to a
  non-tank inside the first 30 s, mostly the first 5 s, while his holder had next to no threat (the
  harness engages at 0) or walked him through the ranged formation to the spot; a 10-man wipe lost two
  ranged that way. A dps bot holds at 90% of the holder's threat and resumes under 80%; a holder still at
  0 holds them all. The multiplier vetoes damage aimed at XT and area damage within 25 yd of him
  standing; the priority action stops what carries no action (swing, Auto Shot, wand, channel) and keeps
  him selected. Submerged he is out of every splash, so the adds walking in to him get the raid's AoE.
  Soulshatter stays allowed, since it halves the bot's threat. The brake reads XT's threat list, never
  the `threat` value, a uint8 share that wraps past 255%.
- **Healer tank buffs follow the holder too** (`GetXT002BuffTank`, user call 2026-11-22). The class nodes
  aim Beacon, Sacred Shield, Earth Shield and the priest's tank shield and Renew at the flagged main tank,
  the paladin's pair at whichever tank dropped lowest (`tank to beacon`, sticky): over 25-man off-tank
  holds of 7-8 min Earth Shield sat 0% on the holder, and with the main tank holding Beacon sat 73% on the
  other tank. So the `xt002 holder *` rows cast them on the holder at their class nodes' relevance, and
  `xt002 class tank buffs` blocks those nodes through XT's combat. Beacon, Sacred Shield and Earth Shield
  are single-target per caster (`SPELL_ATTR5_LIMIT_N`), so a move strips the old tank; to stop pull-time
  victim flips (every 1-3 s) bouncing them, the buff tank is latched per instance and moves only once the
  new holder has kept XT 5 s (`_BUFF_TANK_SETTLE_MS`), at once if the old one is dead. The latch drops
  after 10 s with nobody asking (`_BUFF_TANK_STALE_MS`), so it never outlives its pull. A buff counts
  as already up by this healer's own copy for Beacon and Renew and by anyone's for the shields. The
  Power Word: Shield row is disc only, like the class node: a holy priest's shield would lock the
  disc's out with Weakened Soul.
- **The last tank standing taunts the Pummeller but never targets it.** Taunt reaches 30 yd, so the
  add walks to the tank instead of the tank dragging XT 80 yd to it. Only a tank with another on XT
  targets Pummellers.
- **Ranged and healers get a slot each, never the anchor itself.** Sharing one coordinate put 13
  living bots inside 3 yd of it, four dying on the exact point — Searing Light is 8 yd, so that is the
  whole group. `GetXT002RangedSlot` deals slots off an ellipse (half-axes 6×8 inner, 9×12 outer),
  wider north-south (the layout never rotates: slot 1 sits on the bearing between two fixed points,
  nearest XT and the tank spot, which stands just behind XT's spawn) because east-west is the line to XT and to the tank spot, so spreading along it
  costs range and across it costs nothing. It is centred **6 yd north of the anchor**: on the anchor,
  the southern slots sit 14 yd from the nearest parking cell, inside Gravity Bomb's 20 yd pull, and an
  expiring puddle would drag those bots into it. Offset, every slot is inside 30 yd of XT and 39 yd of
  the tank and 23 yd clear of the nearest cell. Sorted healers-first then guid, so healers take the
  centre and inner ring and every bot derives the same layout untold. The rings limit a splash rather
  than dodge one: the outer clears 8 yd and the inner not quite, since 14 bots cannot all sit 8 yd apart
  and in range, so one splash costs two or three bots, not the group. Slots in Consumption are dealt
  *out of the list*, not stepped around, or two bots pick the same one; a bot left without a clear slot
  keeps its own, and the hazard dodge moves it. The dead keep their slots: indexing by the living
  re-seats everyone behind a corpse mid-fight. Melee get no slot, since pinning them costs uptime on
  a boss that moves. The band a bot may drift off its spot is `ULDUAR_XT002_MAINTANK_SPOT_TOLERANCE`
  (3 yd); with a slot each it only absorbs drift, and widening it stacks them on one point.
  `GetXT002OtherFormationSlots` answers with slots, not positions: a carrier picks
  its destination while the raid is still walking.
- **One healer walks out to a Gravity Bomb carrier beyond every healer's reach**
  (`GetXT002BombHealCarrier`, user call 2026-11-22). Lots sit 42-52 yd from the nearest healer slot
  against 38.5 yd of heal range (`Playerbots.HealDistance`), and the healer pin zeroes `reach party
  member to heal` all fight: over three 25-man pulls 22 of 72 carriers went out of every healer's
  reach, and 3 of the 4 the bomb killed (14.5k, then the tantrum) at 36-86% were among them. The
  nearest bot healer not carrying a debuff is latched per carrier until the bomb is off, so only one
  goes and the pick can't flip; a real player in range counts as cover, and a healer carrying the bomb
  covers itself. `xt002 bomb heal` (`ACTION_RAID + 2`, above every heal) walks it to 5 yd inside heal
  range on the line to the carrier, or up to 45° off it, 20 yd (the bomb pull) off every carrier and
  the hazard dodge's clearance off every Void Zone. The anchor stands down for it until the bomb is
  off, then walks it back. No other healer stands down for a heal target out of range: with the pin,
  that only froze it off its slot.
- **The sent healer heals its carrier first until it's above 90%** (`GetXT002BombHealTarget`,
  `_BOMB_HEAL_TOP_PCT`, user call 2026-11-22), then as usual; the other healers keep their targets.
  The class picker takes whoever is lowest in range, so a carrier at 60-85% loses to any lower tank, and
  the bomb plus the tantrum have killed from 86%. `xt002 bomb carrier heal` (`ACTION_RAID + 1.5`, above
  every heal and tank buff) casts instants first (Power Word: Shield, Riptide, Holy Shock,
  Rejuvenation, Swiftmend): the healer often still walks its last 5 yd, and no cast-time spell goes
  off while moving.
- **Hunters stand on an arc, not in the ring.** The Heart's combat reach is 22.5 (model 28887), so a
  hunter's shot minimum (5 yd plus melee range) is 30.3 yd from it, past every ring slot (12-29 yd
  from XT). From a slot every shot failed `TOO_CLOSE`: the hunter meleed, disengaged out and the anchor
  walked it straight back. MM and survival hunters put 60-210k on the Heart per window against a
  ranged median of ~480k, about the margin it survived by. The arc is 37 yd off XT's spawn (30.3 plus
  the 5 yd slot tolerance), bearings 175°, 165°, 155°, then a row 6 yd out; south of it the Searing Light
  spot comes inside its 12 yd clearance, north of it the northern lot inside the 20 yd bomb pull. A
  puddle on a spot sends that hunter one row out. The ring is dealt without hunters, and
  `GetXT002OtherFormationSlots` lists the arc spots too.
- **The Heart is hidden, not despawned**, when its window shuts (`UNIT_FLAG_NOT_SELECTABLE`,
  `ACTION_DISPOSE_HEART`). `IsAllowedTarget` rejects untargetable units and requires the Exposed Heart
  aura, and the sticky rule in `ResolveTarget` drops a target the gates reject: checking only
  `IsAlive()` left a bot on it for the rest of the fight with every queued spell failing.

## One mover per bot: the carrier and hazard nodes are each merged

Two nodes that can both move the same bot will tie on relevance and fight over the queue, so
`xt002 debuff carrier` fires on either debuff and resolves one destination per tick, and
`xt002 avoid hazard` clears Boombots and Void Zones in one move.

- **Carriers are held by the multiplier, not by returning `true`.** Both carrier actions return
  `false` on arrival, so the tick continued and `reach melee` at `ACTION_HIGH + 1` walked the carrier
  back mid-debuff. `XT002TargetGuardMultiplier` zeroes generic movers for anyone carrying either
  debuff, any role, while the encounter's own movers and every cast keep running — a carrier action
  returning `true` would starve everything below `ACTION_EMERGENCY + 1` for 9-10 s and mute a
  debuffed healer. `AttackAction` stays exempt: it never moves the bot, and zeroing it kills the
  encounter's own targeting.
- **Gap closers move a carrier without being movers.** Charge and intercept derive from
  `CastReachTargetSpellAction`, not `MovementAction`, so the stand-down never saw them and no
  multiplier had ever vetoed one: parked carriers charged back out of the lot three times in one pull,
  once into a puddle they then died in and twice 20 yd into the melee stack holding a live bomb, whose
  expiry yanks everything inside 20 yd. Zeroed for carriers only, so melee keep their opener.
- **Gravity Bomb outranks Searing Light on a double carrier.** The puddle denies raid floor for 180 s;
  the 12 yd splash lasts 9 s and expires over an empty lot. The Life Spark then spawns out there and
  walks in by itself. Reversed, a 180 s puddle lands on the one spot reserved for sparks.
- **The carrier keeps ownership while it holds either debuff.** When the bomb expires with Searing
  Light still ticking, the bot is standing in its own fresh puddle — which makes its old cell test
  occupied, so the picker moves it 6-12 yd to the next free one. Only a bot that held the bomb under
  its current Searing Light steps off in the lot: a lone Searing Light carrier dragged into the lot's
  box by another bomb's pull parked there, and its spark spawned among the melee (27 s, 3 dead).
- **The hazard node ignores Void Zones while the bot carries a debuff** and always honours Boombots.
  Cell selection already owns where a carrier stands relative to puddles; letting the generic dodge
  fire would fling it up to 30 yd in whatever direction was emptiest.
- **Hazard moves take the nearest sufficient point, not the furthest** — `MoveClearOf`'s ring search
  stops at the first spot that clears everything, unlike `MoveAwayFromCreatureAction`'s
  maximise-distance sweep. It takes per-unit clearances, so one search serves the pre-Heartbreak ally
  spread at 25 yd and the mixed hazard list of Boombots at 12 and Void Zones at 8. When no spot clears
  everyone, as in a packed 25-man, it takes any gain over standing still.
- **`avoid aoe` is zeroed for everyone during XT's combat**
  ([pitfalls](../../engine/pitfalls.md#things-the-bot-cannot-see)). It sits at the hazard node's
  relevance and flees a flat `FleeDistance` its own way, so the bot would slide between the two.
  Nothing is lost: Tympanic Tantrum is room-wide so it exceeds `maxAoeAvoidRadius`, and the Life Spark
  has no damage aura.
- **Healers are in the generic-mover stand-down too.** `follow` lives on the non-combat engine and a
  healer with nothing to heal drops combat constantly — 973 of 2857 actions in one pull, against 1.7%
  for ranged — so it out-issued the anchor three to one and walked them to the master all fight.
  Their disperse goes with it; the anchor's slots are what spreads them now.
- **Healers keep a target, and nothing here may clear it.** A bot with no target never attacks, never
  enters combat, and so runs the non-combat engine — for a priest that is renew, penance and greater
  heal, with no Power Word: Shield, Prayer of Mending, Pain Suppression, Shadowfiend or Hymn of Hope,
  and for a paladin no Beacon of Light. Clearing healer targets left all four targetless for 100% of
  a pull against ~33% on other Ulduar bosses, with not one shield or Beacon even evaluated. Ulduar is
  in `RestrictedHealerDPSMaps` so the target costs no GCD; what it buys is `Attack`'s
  `ChangeEngine(BOT_STATE_COMBAT)`. `dps assist` is exempted for healers for the same reason.

## Nothing here is reachable just because it is on the mesh

Two silent failures, both measured from one wipe trace: the parking lot was chosen **0 times in 161
carrier moves**, and Searing Light dealt 1.53M damage to bystanders against 298k to carriers.

- **`IsWithinLOS` rejects the entire parking lot.** The building geometry ends at y ≈ −29 (vmap
  surface ~404 under the raid, none south of it) and the lot sits past that edge on bare terrain, so
  a ray from the raid clips the rim while the path to every cell is `PATHFIND_NORMAL`. **No XT-002
  mover has an LOS test any more** — the lot, the ring search and the stop-short point are all
  validated by `MoveTo`'s outcome instead. On the ring search the gate was pruning 820 of 823 ticks
  down to one candidate, which leaves the retry loop nothing to retry.
- **`findSmoothPath` refuses walkable points, and refuses the same ones every tick.** A sunken WMO
  roof underlies the yard and rises east — vmap 393 at x = 865, 405 at x = 890, emerging as a 411 step
  at x ≈ 900 — so the mesh carries two layers and paths across it drop to Z 398–406 and oscillate.
  **The refusal follows where the bot stands, not where it is going**: from the ranged formation
  (x ≤ 872) every northern point answers `PATHFIND_NORMAL`, while from the melee stack (x ≥ 875)
  everything north of y ≈ +3 answers `PATHFIND_SHORTCUT|PATHFIND_NOPATH` and the southern and western
  arcs are clean. Within that split it is arbitrary: on a 34 yd ring around XT, headings 0/45/60/135°
  fail and 30/90/120/150/180° succeed with the mesh 8/8 present. `SearchForBestPath` accepts only
  `PATHFIND_NORMAL|INCOMPLETE`, so `MoveTo` returns false and the bot does not move **at all** — for
  the full 9 s debuff, since re-offering one winner re-offers the same refusal. The Searing Light
  spot has to serve carriers from both stacks, which is what puts it south-west; it used to be
  **(862.737, 12.779, 409.832)**, on the mesh and never once reached. Do not re-pick a point because
  navprobe says it is walkable.

So every mover here ranks its candidates and offers them in turn, up to
`ULDUAR_XT002_MOVE_CANDIDATE_ATTEMPTS` (each try costs up to `Playerbots.MaxMovementSearchTime` path
builds), through `MovementAction::TryMoveTo`: `MoveTo`'s bool collapses
`NoPath` into the same false as `Duplicate` and `Waiting`, and reading those as failure turns a
carrier around mid-run. Only `NoPath` earns another candidate; `NotAllowed` means yield the tick.

## The parking lot is a time budget, not a coordinate

Before Heartbreak no puddle drops, so a bomb carrier doesn't park: it only clears every ally by
`ULDUAR_XT002_DEBUFF_CLEAR_RADIUS` (25 yd, twice the 12 yd splash, since a bomb at the edge still
clips whoever drifted a yard), which keeps melee uptime.

Three grids walking +x from a raid-facing origin and then away from the raid in y, so every added cell
is further out: 5×4 for each ranged lot, 7×4 for the melee one, all 68 navprobe-verified on mesh. Step
is just over the Void Zone diameter, so consecutive drops cannot overlap. `GetXT002BombLots` is the
single source of which grids a role owns, so the mover and the "am I in a lot" test cannot disagree.
The Void Zone scan is anchored on XT (`_VOID_ZONE_SEARCH_RADIUS` 110) against a furthest cell 92 yd
from XT's most easterly measured position: a puddle outside the radius is a cell the ranking believes
is free.

- **Ranged and healers get a lot each side of the formation; melee get one.** The formation is 24 yd
  deep, so a single lot has half of it walking 46 yd where the other half walks 23 — and Tympanic
  Tantrum halves movement, cutting the reach to ~25 yd, which is one lot away and no further. The
  northern origin is the southern one reflected about the formation centre, so both sit 23.2 yd off
  the nearest slot, outside Gravity Bomb's 20 yd pull either side.
- **Melee get no northern lot, and distance rules it out even where the mesh does not.** Everything
  north of the boss line is `NOPATH` from their stack; the route that does path runs west then north,
  and the nearest northern point that both paths and clears the 20 yd pull off the raid is 53 yd out
  against a 50.5 yd budget, 26 yd of it under the tantrum. Their lot grows **east** instead, 7 columns
  to x 907.5, probed flat at 409.803 and `PATHFIND_NORMAL` out to x 913 with `NOPATH` at 925. Width is
  the point: a lot is only ever entered from the raid side, so a row of puddles across it seals
  everything behind, and three at y around -47 left no cell in the 5-wide lot with a clean walk in.
  Not west, where a column at 865.5 would sit 4.6 yd off the ranged-south lot's east column and steal
  its cells. 18-44 yd.
- **Ranking is reachability first**, inside the aura's remaining duration at the bot's current speed,
  then how clear the walk in is, then, for a live bomb, clearing every other raider by the 20 yd pull,
  then room at the cell, then the shortest walk — that last one is what sends a ranged carrier to the
  near lot rather than across the room. `GetSpeed(MOVE_RUN)` already carries the tantrum slow, so no
  encounter code has to know the tantrum exists. The budget takes `ULDUAR_XT002_BOMB_TRAVEL_MARGIN_MS`
  (1.5 s) off the debuff for reaction delay, one tick and a path longer than the straight line. Only a
  bot actually holding the bomb is budgeted — stepping off its own puddle is a short hop with no
  deadline.
- **The raid tier is for the melee lot.** With XT on the tank spot, melee on his south side stand
  10-15 yd from its first row: over nine 25-man pulls 26 of 46 first-row bombs had a raider inside the
  12 yd hit and 39 inside the 20 yd pull, which drags melee into the lot, where puddles trapped them
  for the next carrier (one bomb: 6 hit, 2 dead). Ranked under the walk it adds no puddle crossing;
  replayed on 72 melee bombs, raiders inside the hit fell from 59 to 8.
- **When nothing is reachable the carrier stops short**, keeping the bearing to the best cell and
  walking as far as the budget allows, but only if that point is clear of every live puddle by
  `ULDUAR_XT002_VOID_ZONE_RADIUS` and at least `ULDUAR_XT002_GRAVITY_BOMB_PULL_RADIUS` (20 yd, the real
  pull radius: the expiry pull 63025 / 64233 effect 1, while the damage only reaches 12 yd and the pull
  into a fresh Void Zone is what kills) from every living raider — so the puddle lands on the approach instead of in the raid.
  Otherwise the ring search takes the tick, **with the puddles in its avoid list**: a slowed carrier
  that can reach neither a cell nor a stop-short point ended a pull 1.7 yd from a live puddle when that
  search cleared only the raid.
  The point needs no latch: as the bot advances, reach and distance to the cell shrink together.
- **Rank the walk by how close it comes, never yes/no** (`_BOMB_APPROACH_CLEARANCE` 7.5 as the cap,
  `_BOMB_CELL_PREFERRED_CLEARANCE` 8.0 at the cell, hard floor 6.0). As a gate the cell preference
  would block five cells of twenty and exhaust the lot; as a gate the approach margin picked the
  *worse* walk, because 7.5 sits above Consumption's 5 yd while cells sit 6 yd apart, so two occupied
  cells in a row made every remaining cell in it read "not clear" at a distance that costs nothing.
  One carrier was sent on a 30 yd walk passing 4.0 yd from a puddle over a 19 yd one passing 7.3, and
  took 37.7k of that pull's 72.9k Consumption by itself. Capping at 7.5 leaves every genuinely clear
  walk tied, so a clean room ranks exactly as it did. There is no puddle dodging on the way in — the
  carrier sits at `ACTION_EMERGENCY + 1` and starves the Void Zone node — and the segment test is an
  approximation, since `MoveTo` follows a navmesh path rather than the line measured.
- **`ParkVoidZone` reports on cells, not on `MoveTo`** — "already walking there" is not "nowhere to
  go", and "no path to there" is a fourth answer meaning try the next cell. Sapphiron's
  `ShelterResult` shape otherwise: latch on arrival, `StopMoving()`, deadband 2.0, re-engage 5.0,
  tighter than Sapphiron's because the puddle lands at the carrier's feet and the destination is
  static. Arrival is measured against the nearest free cell, not the best-ranked one, so a bot on a
  cell has parked whatever the ranking prefers elsewhere — but standing in Consumption is never
  parked, whatever the bands say, since a displaced carrier can sit inside a puddle and still measure
  within the re-engage band of some other cell. While the best-ranked cell is reachable and clears the
  raid, both bands count only cells that do, or a carrier bound for the third row parks on the
  first-row cell it crosses. Reachable too: in the bomb's last ~2 s nothing is, and a carrier parked
  inside some raider's 20 yd would unpark into the ring search. Both bands stay under the grid step,
  so only one cell can answer. The parked carrier then yields the tick, so a healer can heal and a
  hunter can shoot XT from the lot. A Searing Light carrier stepping off its own bomb with every cell
  taken holds where it is: the splash expires over an empty lot anyway.
- **The Searing Light spot needs alternates, ranked away from the raid.** A Void Zone parked 2.1 yd
  from it and stayed for the last 133 s of a pull, and carriers kept being sent in. The spot is
  `(846, −22, 409.6)`, south-west: 15.7 yd from the nearest formation slot and 19.2 yd from the
  nearest parking cell, so the 8 yd splash reaches neither, and 38 yd from the melee stack, inside the
  9 s run. The carrier takes it, or a point on a 10 yd ring that is puddle-free, pathable, and
  `ULDUAR_XT002_SEARING_LIGHT_SLOT_CLEARANCE` (12 yd) clear of every slot; the ring clears the Void
  Zone radius, so one puddle can't cover spot and ring. **Rank by clearance, never by distance from
  the bot**: nearest-first returns the headings pointing back at the raid, and carriers took those 132
  times in one pull with 4–6 raiders inside the splash every time. A fixed destination here is
  checked against the formation as well as against puddles — the two are laid out independently and
  will drift into each other.
- **Searing Light hurts the raid on the walk, not at the destination — so rank the route, not the
  endpoint.** Of 1,030,308 total, **568,592 landed on raiders other than the carrier, 100% of it while
  the carrier was still moving and none once parked**, 61% inside the first quarter of the run. All 24
  runs passed 1-17 raiders through the 8 yd splash while **23 of 24 destinations were clean**, so
  endpoint clearance was already solved and is not where the damage is. Ranking candidate spots by
  *how many raiders the run passes* instead replays at **161 → 102 crossings (−37%)** for 52 extra
  yards of walking. The second spot that does it is `(900, 26)` — on mesh at 409.811 with 8/8 of its
  ring on mesh — and `(900, 36)` is the only north-east point reachable from the melee stack.

## Traps

- **Don't split `XT002TargetGuardMultiplier` into rules.** It was checked equivalent to the guards it
  replaced as one unit, over an 18.5 M-input
  [harness](../../engine/raid-mechanics-lessons.md#measuring-a-cost); a split needs that check redone.
- **A bubble drops the puddle early.** Divine Shield (642) and Ice Block (45438) both apply
  `SPELL_AURA_SCHOOL_IMMUNITY` over every magic school with `SPELL_ATTR1_IMMUNITY_PURGES_EFFECT`, and
  `spell_xt002_gravity_bomb_aura::OnRemove` has no removal-mode check — so a paladin or mage that
  bubbles while carrying summons its Void Zone on the spot. Bots cast both from "critical health" at
  relevance 90, which arrives during a Tympanic Tantrum: exactly when the carrier is still in the raid
  and cannot walk out. `XT002TargetGuardMultiplier` zeroes both while the bot carries either debuff;
  Searing Light is in the same school mask and drops its Life Spark the same way. Hand of Protection
  (1022) is physical-only and strips neither; Anti-Magic Shell blocks the bomb landing rather than
  dropping one.
- **Hand of Freedom cannot help a carrier outrun the tantrum.** 62775 carries `Mechanic = 0` and no
  effect mechanic, while Hand of Freedom is keyed to `MECHANIC_ROOT` and `MECHANIC_SNARE` — same for
  the PvP trinket, Every Man for Himself and shapeshift. Flat speed buffs do work (Sprint gives
  5.25 y/s against 3.5), but only some classes carry one and the carrier is whoever the boss picked.
- **Bots do not dodge Life Sparks and cannot.** Static Charged (64227) is an enemy area aura at radius
  index 30 — **500 yd** — so position changes nothing, and no avoid node can match it. What looked
  like dodging was melee making the 34 yd round trip to the spark.
- **A Life Spark that can't path to its victim is immune until it can.** The chase sets
  `CanNotReachTarget`; `IsEvadingAttacks` then zeroes every hit and turns a taunt into an evade miss,
  and in a raid map `EvadeTimerExpired` only keeps it regenerating: no reset, no threat dropped. Its
  Static Charged (25-man 64236, 2000 per 3 s on every raider) ticks on. One dropped by the off-tank on
  the step at (898, −4) sat 63 s at full health with nine bots shooting it, until its victim walked
  off a north formation slot; a second from the same tank was left to cleave, and three sparks up at
  once wiped the raid. So the priority action skips an evading add (`IsXT002AddEvading`) and never
  keeps one as its pick of that type, which would hide the hittable spark beside it. Tanks don't take
  sparks.
- **Searing Light stays carrier-only, and bystanders eat it.** It damages allies within 8 yd every
  second for 9 s, nothing steps out of it, and generic `avoid aoe` cannot see an aura a player carries
  ([pitfalls](../../engine/pitfalls.md#things-the-bot-cannot-see)). The carrier needs 4-6 s to clear,
  and a Pummeller off-tank irradiates the melee stack for the whole debuff because it stays put.
  Accepted: 25 bots scattering costs more than the damage.
- **Tanks stay put for Searing Light while holding XT or a Pummeller** — dragging either costs more
  than the splash — but they do park for Gravity Bomb, and a Pummeller following an off-tank into the
  lot is fine. The core never gives either debuff to XT's victim. After Heartbreak no adds come, so a
  tank holding neither, the flagged main tank included, carries Searing Light to the spot like anyone
  (user call, 2026-11-22). Left in place, its spark spawned in the melee stack where it couldn't path
  to its victim (above). Like any carrier, one that held the bomb under this Searing Light keeps to
  its lot, so a spark can still spawn at the melee lot, outside the reach the raid takes sparks from
  (26-39 s alive).
- `MoveAwayFromPlayerWithDebuffAction` takes a **single** spell id fixed at construction, so it cannot
  cover both the 10 and 25-man ids of Searing Light or Gravity Bomb.
- **A trigger and its action must measure a hazard the same way.** `TooCloseToCreature` asks
  `FindNearestCreature`, whose range test subtracts both object sizes; `MoveClearOf` scores centre to
  centre and caps at zero. In the gap between them the trigger says "too close" while no candidate
  beats standing still, so the action fails every tick — and `XT002RaidPositionTrigger` yields to that
  trigger, so the anchor cannot recover the bot either. One ranged bot sat 64.7 yd from XT for 125 s
  that way, casting nothing, until it died. So the dodge clears to Void Zone radius + 2, leaving
  headroom, and the anchor yields only while the bot is inside the raw radius by the action's own
  measure. The radius itself is `ULDUAR_XT002_VOID_ZONE_RADIUS` 6: Consumption (64208) is 5 yd and
  doesn't grow, plus a 1 yd buffer.
- **`XT002TargetGuardMultiplier` runs on every action every bot pops anywhere in Ulduar**, and finding
  XT is a scan, so it sorts the action by kind first and looks XT up only for the kinds that can
  answer anything but 1.0; the two aura-map walks sit behind the mover and immunity branches too.
- **Two carriers can pick the same cell** — there is no reservation. Bombs are 16 s apart against a
  9 s duration so two bomb carriers never overlap, and the only bot that can collide is one sitting
  out a Searing Light, which drops nothing.

## What a trace answers

`tools/botobs/bosses/xt_002_deconstructor.py <trace>`:

- `--heart`: each window, Heartbreak, and the hunters' Heart damage against the ranged median.
- `--adds`: adds per toy pile. `--trash`: Scrapyard packs and the Pummeller that called them.
- `--sparks`: sparks that lived 20 s or more, with their carrier and evade notes, and each spark a
  tank dropped with its distance from the Searing Light spot.
- `--tanks`: who XT was on and that tank's distance off the tank spot, his time on non-tanks, duty
  notes, taunts cast at XT, the dps brake's holds, the buff tank, and each healer tank buff's share
  of the 15 s+ holds on the holder against the other tank.
- `--bombs`: each Gravity Bomb carrier that went out of every healer's reach or died to its bomb, the
  healer sent, how close it got, when it was in range, its heals and shields on the carrier, the
  carrier's hp just before the bomb hit and its walk back, then the sent carriers' hp at the hit
  against 90%, and the raiders other carriers' bombs hit after Heartbreak, the melee lot's (melee and
  tank carriers) apart.
- `--deaths`: deaths by mechanic.

Probes: `xt002.slot` (each bot's formation or hunter slot), `xt002.evading` (the sparks a bot sees
as unhittable, `none` once they aren't), `xt002.tank` (each tank's duty: `holds XT`, `pummellers`,
both or `none`), `xt002.buffs` (the latched buff tank), `xt002.brake` (`1` while a dps bot holds its
damage on XT), `xt002.bombheal` (per bomb carrier, the healer sent; `0` once let go).
